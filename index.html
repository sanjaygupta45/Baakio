Quick answers

Before you start.

Baakio is designed for everyday credit, partial payments and payment promises.

What is Baakio?

A simple customer credit and payment tracker. It keeps purchases, confirmed payments, remaining balance and promises together.

Can customers pay in parts?

Yes. Record every confirmed payment in the same account. Baakio shows the amount still left.

Does Baakio send WhatsApp reminders automatically?

No. Baakio prepares a message for you. You review it and choose whether to send it from WhatsApp.
