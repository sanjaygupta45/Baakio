Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers.

What is Baakio?

Baakio is a credit and payment tracker for businesses. It brings each customer's purchases, payments, remaining balance and payment promises into one account.

Can I track customers who buy often and pay in parts?

Yes. Add every purchase and confirmed payment to the same customer account. Baakio shows what is still outstanding.

Can AI read handwritten bills?

The planned AI scan can prepare an editable draft from a photo of a new bill. It may make mistakes, so you must check and approve the details before they are saved.

Will Baakio send WhatsApp reminders automatically?

No. Baakio prepares a message with the customer's balance, next promise and your confirmed UPI details. You review it and send it from WhatsApp yourself.

Can I use Baakio in Hindi?

Yes. The planned app supports English and Hindi. You can also choose the language of a customer's reminder separately from your app language.
